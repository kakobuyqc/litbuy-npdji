What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize potential finds, source links, prices, sizes and comparison notes. Treat it as a starting point: an entry does not verify product quality, authenticity, stock or whether a platform will accept the item.

The research workflow

A useful list needs more than links.

Organize your Litbuy product finds around information you can verify. A short, well-documented list is easier to use than hundreds of unexplained product URLs.

Keep the browsing page and the original source URL in separate columns. They may serve different purposes when you check purchasing options.

Start browsing CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose a specific category

    Begin with a need: a lightweight jacket, an everyday T-shirt or a matching set. Narrow by fit, material and intended use before comparing prices.

  2. Save the exact product and variant

    Record the source URL, seller, selected color, size and displayed price. Note when you checked the listing because details can change.

  3. Compare measurements and evidence

    Use garment measurements and size charts. Compare available QC photos with the selected variant and write down anything that remains unclear.

  4. Check the purchase route and total

    Confirm current platform acceptance, applicable fees, delivery options and return conditions. Only then decide whether the item belongs on your final shortlist.

Your comparison framework

Know what each column is telling you.

Use this structure for Litbuy spreadsheet finds without confusing a research note with a verified product claim.

Suggested fields for a personal product research spreadsheet
Field What to record What to verify
Product identity Item name, seller, original URL and chosen variant. The link still opens the intended product.
Size and fit Size label, garment measurements or foot-length guidance. The listing's chart matches the selected version.
Materials Listed fabric composition, finish and care details. The seller supplies enough information to assess the claim.
Photos and QC Photo source, visible condition and unresolved questions. Images show the relevant item and variant.
Estimated cost Item price, delivery, fees and possible import charges. Currency, shipping quote and final checkout amounts.
Decision status Researching, awaiting details, shortlisted or removed. Your reason for keeping or rejecting the item.

A low listed price is only one part of the decision. Fit, missing information and delivery costs can change the overall value.

Plan before checkout

Give your shortlist a realistic budget.

A useful Litbuy shopping guide separates the product subtotal from the cost of getting the items to you. Domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping and import charges may all affect the final amount.

Enter your own estimates in one currency. The calculator adds them together; it does not retrieve live prices, exchange rates, tax rules or shipping quotes.

Questions worth answering.

Clear answers for using a product list as a research tool and understanding what this page provides.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize potential finds, source links, prices, sizes and comparison notes. A listing in a spreadsheet does not verify quality, authenticity, availability or platform acceptance.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a shopping research guide and links to CAFANS product categories. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Litbuy product feed.

How do I check whether a product link works with Litbuy?

Check the original product URL using Litbuy's current interface or support before paying. A CAFANS category link is a browsing destination and is not a guarantee that a particular source link can be purchased through Litbuy.

What should I check in QC photos?

Compare the selected color, size label, measurements, stitching, visible condition and included items with the listing. QC photos can reveal visible issues, but they do not prove authenticity, durability or comfort.

How do I estimate the total cost of a shopping shortlist?

Add product prices, domestic delivery, service and payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Keep all inputs in the same currency and confirm actual charges before payment.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent guide linking to CAFANS. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Brand mentions and editorial images do not certify the authenticity of any linked product.
